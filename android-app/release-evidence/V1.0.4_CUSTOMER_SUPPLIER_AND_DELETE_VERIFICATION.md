## 1. Executive Summary

Ledger v1.0.4 delivers two major architectural capabilities while maintaining 100% backward compatibility, local-first reliability, zero regressions to existing production data, and exact release cryptographic continuity:

1. **Customer & Supplier Ledger Separation:**
   - Dual-mode segmented toggle (`Customers | Suppliers`) positioned immediately above the search bar on the home screen with $\ge 48\text{dp}$ touch targets.
   - Distinct entity domain roles (`CUSTOMER` vs `SUPPLIER`) stored in Room Database (Schema version 6).
   - Full phone number independence: A customer and a supplier can share the exact same mobile number (e.g., `9888888888`) with zero collision, merging, or cross-contamination.
   - Dedicated supplier accounting:
     - **GOODS RECEIVED (RECEIVE):** Goods or credit received from supplier (Increases payable / "You Owe").
     - **YOU PAID (GIVE):** Payment made to supplier (Decreases payable / Reduces "You Owe").
     - **Net Supplier Payable:** $\sum \text{RECEIVE} - \sum \text{GIVE}$ ($> 0$: You Owe; $< 0$: Advance; $= 0$: Settled).
     - **Dashboard Card 1 (Total Payable):** Non-offsetting aggregation $\sum \max(\text{Net Payable}, 0)$ so supplier advances never offset debts owed to other suppliers.
     - **Dashboard Card 2 (Paid Today):** Total active payments made to suppliers on the current local calendar day (excluding deleted payments).
2. **Delete Individual Transactions:**
   - Accessible `MoreVert` overflow menu on every transaction item row with "Edit Transaction" and "Delete Transaction".
   - Explicit confirmation dialog displaying party name, transaction type, formatted amount, and an immediate balance recalculation warning.
   - Non-destructive soft-deletion via `isDeleted = 1`, tombstone generation in `sync_operations`, immediate reactive balance recalculation, and complete preservation of on-disk receipt images.
3. **Backup & Restore Schema v4:**
   - JSON backup schema bumped to version 4 with explicit `role` serialization.
   - Seamless backward compatibility with legacy schema versions 1–3 (defaulting unassigned entities to `CUSTOMER`).
   - Round-trip restoration preserves roles, relations, balances, tombstones, recurring rules, and bill receipt byte payloads.
